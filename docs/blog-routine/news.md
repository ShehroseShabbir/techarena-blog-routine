# News run (Monday)

Follow this file, then `docs/blog-routine/common.md` for every step (identity, weekly slot, duplicate check, research rules, writing, 60/155 pre-check, create, retry, summary). Read `docs/blog-drafting-brief.md` for voice and structure.

## Task

Write one news article: a current technology, AI, Microsoft 365 or security story, framed as "what it means for your business".

- Category: News & Events, slug `news-events`, id 8. Use `categories: [8]` in the create POST and the same id in the weekly-slot query.
- Story: published or announced in the last 14 days, preferring the last 7 (brief §4.1), reported by at least two sources, one of them primary (primary sources as in brief §7, for example CISA or NIST). Prefer stories that change what an Australian small or medium business should do, check or decide. Skip rumours, unconfirmed leaks and anything only a single secondary outlet reports.
- Angle: say what happened in plain words, who is affected, what it means for a business like the reader's, and the one to three actions worth taking this week. No fear language, no invented statistics, and no claims about Tech Arena that the brief does not allow.
- Use the structure the brief sets for news posts, with a public "Sources" list and the editor-notes block at the end.
- If the topic overlaps an existing post or own draft, pick another story (common.md step 3).

Then continue at common.md step 1 and finish with its run summary.
